### LESSON 1.0.2 - Human Psychology

**Ignore everything you have ever learned about human psychology. Let's begin from first principles.**

#### Biological Imperative
The biological imperative asserts that every living being exists to pass on its genes.
- → This is the fundamental unit of action that drives every living being
- → Everything we know as human psychology is an abstraction of this concept
- → Maslow's hierarchy of needs is the best nuanced representation

**The takeaway:** Survival is solved. Increase health, status, meaning, and quality/quantity of romantic partners.

#### Solve the Biggest Problems First
It's almost always easier to solve **10% of a major problem** than **100% of a minor one**.

- **Major problem** → Finding a romantic partner
- **10% solution** → An AI that suggests better dating app responses
- **Minor problem** → Splitting a dinner bill evenly
- **100% solution** → An app that calculates and charges everyone instantly

#### Make People Feel, Not Think
The value of your product should be implicit and self-evident. Identify the fundamental human drive the problem relates to — survival, reproduction, status, security, belonging, etc.

Build as close to that drive as possible. The closer you are, the more natural your growth will be.

### 4.1.0 - What is Influencer Marketing?

**Definition:** The practice of partnering with individuals who have built trust, authority, and a loyal following within a specific niche to promote products, services, or ideas.

**Why it works:**
- **Transfer of Trust** – audiences extend the trust from the influencer to the brand
- **Social Proof** – a respected voice validates the brand's credibility
- **Emotional Connection** – Followers feel like they KNOW the influencer

**Bottom line:** People trust people. Not companies.

### 4.1.2 - How to Source Influencers

**The FYP Method**
1. Create a fresh TikTok account
2. Search, save, and comment on videos that your target user would engage with
3. Remove irrelevant posts from your feed so TikTok learns to only show content related to your product

**The Search & Filter Method**
1. Open TikTok on mobile
2. Use the search bar to find a video your target user might search for
3. Select Videos, then tap Filters
4. Sort by Like Count and Last 3 Months

**The "Related Accounts" Method**
1. On Instagram, go to the influencer's profile
2. Tap the small person icon next to "Message" or "Email"
3. Instagram will show 15+ related accounts in the same niche
4. Repeat for each related account — this creates an endless list of prospects

### 4.1.4 - Negotiations

**The DM Process**
Never share exact pricing upfront. When asked about budget, say:

> "We pay retainers ranging from $500 to $10,000+ depending on the deal we do together."

**Goal:** Move the conversation to a call where you can tailor the deal.

#### Pre-Call Research
- Posting daily with no scattered gaps
- Consistent views of ~100k per video
- Engagement that matches view counts

#### The Pitch Structure
**Offer:** 4 videos/month (1 per week)
**Payment:** Based on consistent views per reel × 4 × $2–$3 CPM

**Example:** If they average 250k views/reel → 1M views/month × $2 CPM = $2,000/month
- 20% paid upfront, remainder paid upon hitting the view guarantee

#### Common Objections & Responses

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "I usually charge $400–$500 per post." | "This is a long-term retainer deal, not a one-off post. You're locking in guaranteed monthly income." |
| "I'm not sure about the view guarantee." | "We base the guarantee on what you already get consistently — it's achievable." |
| "What if I can't get the views?" | "Our rate is based on your consistent average — it's achievable." |
| "What happens if I don't reach the view guarantee?" | "You post again until the views are met." |

### 4.1.7 - Theme Pages

**What Are Theme Pages?**
Theme pages are social accounts that post niche-specific, non-personal content to large audiences:

**Why Theme Pages Work**

**How to Work With Theme Pages**

### Organic Content Types

**Intro: Emotion First**
Any organic content needs to invoke emotion—curiosity, surprise, laughter, FOMO, or even discomfort. Without an emotional trigger, users scroll past.

#### 1. Faceless Content
**Definition:** Content that doesn't feature a visible creator, often repurposed or edited clips.

**Examples:** Podcast edits, trending audio overlays, meme-styled reels, AI-styled edits

**Purpose:** Grab attention fast with trends or provocative ideas

**Best for:** Awareness + virality. Gets eyeballs on your app quickly

**Tactics:**
- Use trending formats/styles for faster traction
- Focus on bold statements, surprising facts, or viral "edits"
- Always include a subtle CTA (e.g., text overlay "Get the app →")

#### 2. UGC (User-Generated Content)
**Definition:** Content made by creators who organically showcase your app in a personal, authentic way

**Types:**
- **Skits / Relatable Stories:** Short skits dramatizing a problem your app solves
- **App Demos / Walkthroughs:** Screen recordings + commentary of using the app
- **Hook-Driven Content:** A big, fast claim in the first 3 seconds, followed by proof/demo

**Best for:** Engagement + conversion. People trust authentic creator voices

**Tactics:**
- Encourage creators to test 2–3 styles on TikTok or IG
- Use hooks like: "This app just saved me X hours…" or "You won't believe what this app does"
- Build micro-trust → app downloads → word-of-mouth growth

#### 3. Reddit-Style Content
**Definition:** Storytelling posts that feel raw, authentic, and unpolished—often text-based or screenshot formats

**Purpose:** Conversion-focused. These feel gritty and real, pushing people down-funnel

**Best for:** Community-driven platforms, Reddit itself, or TikTok/IG posts mimicking Reddit screenshots

**Tactics:**
- Use storytelling: rock-bottom moment → discovery of app → transformation
- Keep it relatable and conversational, not polished
- CTA at the end like "Try it free here" or "This changed everything →"

#### 4. X (Twitter) Content
**Definition:** Short, viral, shareable content that spreads through memes, hot takes, or informative threads

**Types:**
- **Memes:** Relating app benefits to cultural moments or internet humor
- **Data Threads:** "Here's how [X] affects your brain/body/money (with studies)"

**Purpose:** Build authority + reach. Works well for educating and sparking discussion

**Best for:** Driving shares and sparking curiosity that funnels people to your app

**Tactics:**
- Lead with a meme or hot take to grab attention
- Pack a thread with surprising, stat-driven info
- End with a strong CTA like: "That's why I built ___ app. Try it here."

### Closing Note
Each organic content type has its role in the funnel:

- **Faceless** → Awareness (go viral, hit volume)
- **UGC** → Engagement + Trust (show real people using the app)
- **Reddit** → Conversion (gritty, story-driven, persuasive)
- **X** → Authority + Shareability (education, memes, cultural hooks)

**The best strategy isn't picking one—it's layering them** so people encounter your app in different emotional contexts, reinforcing awareness until they download.

### 4.2.3 - Organic Account Setup + Warm Up

Before posting, your account needs to earn trust from TikTok's algorithm so your first videos actually get pushed to the right audience.

#### Step 1: Create Your Account
- Download TikTok and create your account immediately
- **Username:** Choose something that feels authentic and memorable
- **Profile Picture:** Use a clear, well-lit photo of your real face
- **Bio:** Keep it natural and conversational

#### Step 2: Warm-Up Timeline
**Duration:** 3–4 days (minimum)
**Daily Time Commitment:** 10–15 minutes, a few times per day

#### Step 3: Warm-Up Process
**Search & Engage in Your Niche**
Use TikTok search to find content in your niche

**Engage Fully on Posts You See**
On each post: Like, Comment (genuine comments), Save, Share

**Repost & Comment Daily**
- Repost 5–10 videos/day to your own account
- Leave 5–10 thoughtful comments/day on niche videos
- If you see irrelevant content, mark it as "Not Interested"

#### Step 4: Gauge Warm-Up Success
After 3–4 days, check your For You Page (FYP):
- Scroll through 10 videos
- If 8+ are in your niche, your account is warmed up

#### Step 5: Keep It Warm (Maintenance)
Even after posting begins, you must continue acting like a real user:
- Spend time daily engaging with niche videos
- Repost ~5 videos per day, watch them fully, and interact
- Continue liking, saving, and commenting daily

#### Step 6: Posting Rules
- Post 3 videos/day (max 4)
- Space them at least 2 hours apart
- Don't post before 10 AM local time
- Always add captions, hashtags, and on-screen text

**Summary:** Your first 3–4 days on TikTok are not about posting—they're about convincing the algorithm you're a real person in your niche.

- Use natural light or ring lights to make screen demos visible
- Incorporate trending audios only if they fit the emotion
- Demo key features visually
- Make clips long enough for viewers to read on-screen text
- Space out posts by at least 2 hours if uploading multiple
- Engage with comments immediately
- Test multiple creative angles

❌ **DON'T:**
- Run generic viral edits with no app demo — awareness without product is wasted
- Wait until the end to show the app; you'll lose viewers before the CTA
- Film in the dark/night or in front of heavy backlight
- Post all videos at once — let the algo breathe
- Rely only on text overlays to explain the app; you must show it
- Clutter the screen with captions/buttons that cover the demo
- Name-drop the app in the first second — let people feel discovery and curiosity first
- Create overly polished, "ad-looking" creatives — organic should feel authentic

**Quick Formula for a Winning Creative:**
1. **Hook (Emotion):** Call out pain, surprise, or curiosity
2. **Demo (Product):** Show the app solving the problem in real time
3. **CTA (Soft):** "This is what I'm using →" / "Changed everything for me."
