## 🤔 General Questions

### **What is [APP_NAME]?**

[APP_NAME] is a social accountability app where friend groups ("packs") hold each other accountable to daily goals through real consequences.

Miss your morning workout? Your friends can fine you $5 or put you in "phone jail" where Instagram and TikTok get actually blocked for 15-60 minutes.

It's peer pressure, productized.

---

### **How is this different from other habit trackers?**

**Traditional habit trackers:**
- You track alone
- No consequences for missing
- Relies on willpower
- Easy to ignore

**[APP_NAME]:**
- Your friends see everything
- Real consequences (money or phone jail)
- Social pressure keeps you going
- Can't ignore your pack

**Bottom line:** Solo apps rely on motivation. We rely on accountability from people you actually care about.

---

### **Who is this for?**

**Perfect for:**
- High school students trying to balance grades, sports, and social life
- College students who want to hit the gym consistently
- Friend groups who want to improve together
- Anyone who fails at solo habit tracking (16-25 is our sweet spot)
- People who respond to peer pressure (in a good way)
- Groups with shared goals (study groups, sports teams)

**Not for:**
- People who want to track alone
- Those uncomfortable with friends seeing their progress
- Anyone under 13

Think of it like a gym buddy who calls you out when you skip leg day. Harsh? Maybe. Effective? Absolutely.

## 👥 Packs & Groups

A pack is your accountability group. 3-10 friends who hold each other accountable.

Think of it like:
- Your gym crew
- Your study group
- Your boys who keep you honest
- Your accountability circle

**Each pack has:**
- Shared feed (see everyone's check-ins)
- Shared goals (everyone knows your commitments)
- Voting power (activate fines)
- Pack settings (fine amounts, jail times)

---

### **How many packs can I join?**

**Unlimited packs on paid subscription.**

**Most users have:**
- 1 main pack (close friends, multiple goals)
- 1-2 specialized packs (gym-only, study-only)

**You don't need 10 packs.** Quality > quantity.

---

### **Can I have different goals in different packs?**

**Yes!**

Example:
- **Pack 1 (The Boys):** Gym, wake up early, no drinking
- **Pack 2 (Study Squad):** Study sessions, class attendance

Each pack sees only the goals you've shared with them.

---

### **What's the ideal pack size?**

**3-5 people is perfect.**

**Too small (2 people):**
- Not enough social pressure
- No tiebreaker for votes
- Boring feed

**Too big (8+ people):**
- Hard to coordinate
- Less personal
- Feed gets cluttered
- Some people don't engage

**Sweet spot: 3-5 close friends**
- Enough accountability
- Personal connection
- Active engagement
- Manageable

---

### **Can I leave a pack?**

**Yes, anytime.**

1. Open pack
2. Tap "Settings"
3. "Leave Pack"
4. Confirm

**What happens:**
- You're removed immediately
- Your goals are deleted from that pack
- Unpaid fines are forgiven
- Pack members are notified

**No penalty. No judgment. You can always leave.**

---

### **Can I kick someone from my pack?**

**Only if you're the pack admin.**

**Pack Admin = person who created the pack**

**To remove someone:**
1. Pack → Settings → Members
2. Tap their name
3. "Remove from Pack"

**When to remove someone:**
- They're inactive (no check-ins for 2+ weeks)

## 🎯 Goals & Check-Ins

### **How do I create a goal?**

1. Tap "Goals" tab
2. Tap "+" button
3. Fill out:
   - Goal name ("Gym")
   - Emoji (💪)
   - Check-in time (7:00 AM)
   - Days (Mon-Fri)
   - Pack (The Boys)
   - Fine amount ($5)
   - Fine type (Cash or Jail)
4. Tap "Create Goal"

**Your goal is now live.** Check in tomorrow morning at 7:00 AM or face consequences!

---

### **Can I edit a goal after creating it?**

**Yes, but limited:**

**You CAN change:**
- Goal name
- Emoji
- Days of the week
- Active/paused status

**You CANNOT change (would be cheating):**
- Check-in time (locked once created)
- Fine amount (locked once created)
- Fine type (locked once created)

**To change locked settings:**
- Delete goal
- Create new one
- Resets streak (sad, but fair)

---

### **What is a "check-in window"?**

**Your check-in window = 30 minutes BEFORE your goal time.**

**Example:**
- Goal: Gym at 7:00 AM
- Check-in window: 6:30 AM - 7:00 AM
- You can check in anytime in this window

**Why a window?**
- Gives flexibility (got there at 6:45? Check in early)
- Proves you're actually there (not checking in at 3 PM)
- Prevents gaming the system

**After 7:00 AM:** Check-in button disappears. You've missed the goal.

---

### **Do I need to take a photo to check in?**

**No. Photos are optional.**

**Check-in options:**
1. **Tap only:** Just press "Check In" button (default)
2. **With photo:** Take selfie/photo as proof (optional)

**When to use photos:**
- Pack doesn't trust you (lol)
- You want proof (transformation pics)
- Pack requires it (set in pack rules)
- Fun/social (show off gym fit)

**Most people just tap.** It's faster.

---

### **What if I'm late but I still did the thing?**

**Tough luck. You missed the window.**

**Example:**
- Goal: Gym at 7:00 AM
- You got there at 7:15 AM
- Check-in closed at 7:00 AM
- You missed it (even though you went)

**Why so strict?**
- Consistency matters (on time = discipline)
- Prevents excuses ("I was gonna go...")
- The point is showing up ON TIME

**Exception:** If it's genuinely unfair (app crashed, emergency), your pack can vote to forgive the fine.

---

### **Can I check in early?**

**Yes, within the 30-minute window.**

**Example:**
- Goal: Gym at 7:00 AM
- Check-in window opens: 6:30 AM
- You get there at 6:40 AM
- Check in immediately ✓

**Cannot check in BEFORE window** (would be cheating - you're not even there yet).

---

### **What if I'm traveling in a different timezone?**

**Goals are locked to your timezone when created.**

**Example:**
- Created goal in EST: 7:00 AM EST
- Travel to PST (3 hours behind)
- Goal still expects check-in at 7:00 AM EST = 4:00 AM local time

**Solutions:**
1. **Wake up early** (dedication!)
2. **Pause goal** while traveling
3. **Delete and recreate** goal in new timezone (resets streak)

**Future feature:** Auto-adjust for timezone changes.

---

### **Can I pause a goal?**

**Yes!**

**How:**
1. Open goal
2. Tap "..."
3. "Pause Goal"
4. Select dates (vacation, injury, etc.)
5. Confirm

**While paused:**
- No check-ins required
- No fines if you don't check in
- Doesn't break streak
- Pack sees goal is paused

**Use cases:**
- Vacation (can't gym for a week)

---

### **What is a "streak"?**

**Streak = consecutive days of checking in.**

**Example:**
- Monday: Check in ✓ (1 day)
- Tuesday: Check in ✓ (2 days)
- Wednesday: Check in ✓ (3 days)
- Thursday: Miss ❌ (streak breaks → 0)
- Friday: Check in ✓ (1 day - restart)

**Streaks are POWERFUL:**
- Motivating (don't want to break 30-day streak)
- Social proof (flex your 100-day streak)
- Gamified (number goes up = dopamine)

**Longest streak shown on:**
- Your profile
- Goal detail screen
- Pack leaderboard

---

### **Do goals carry over to the next day?**

**No. Each day is independent.**

**Example:**
- Monday: Gym at 7 AM (you check in)
- Tuesday: Gym at 7 AM (new check-in required)
- You must check in EVERY scheduled day

**Not like a to-do list** where you can do Monday's task on Tuesday. Each day is its own commitment.

## 💸 Fines & Consequences

### **How do fines work?**

**Process:**

**1. Someone misses their goal**
- Missed gym at 7:00 AM
- Alert card appears in feed

**2. Pack votes to activate fine**
- Tap "Fine Them"
- Vote yes/no
- Requires MAJORITY (3/5 people in pack)

**3. Fine is activated**
- User is notified
- Fine amount determined ($5)
- Fine type determined (cash or jail)

**4. User pays consequences**
- Cash: Pays via Stripe (later) or marks as paid (honor system now)
- Jail: Serves phone jail time (apps blocked)

**5. Pack sees completion**
- Feed post: "Tom paid the fine"
- Or: "Tom served 30 min jail"

---

### **Who decides the fine amount?**

**Pack settings determine defaults:**
- Set when pack is created
- Can be edited by pack admin

**Example pack settings:**
- Default fine: $5
- Default jail time: 30 minutes
- Range: $1-20 (adults), $1-5 (teens 13-17)

**Vote determines if fine activates**, settings determine the amount.

**Future feature:** Variable fines (bigger fines for repeat offenses).

---

**Yes.**

**How to dispute:**

2. Tap "Dispute Fine"

## 📱 Phone Jail

### **What is phone jail?**

**Phone jail = Your selected apps get blocked for 15-60 minutes as punishment for missing a goal.**

**How it works:**
1. You miss your goal (didn't check in)
2. Pack votes to fine you (phone jail selected)
3. Fine activates → Jail starts
4. Instagram, TikTok, etc. are BLOCKED
5. Timer counts down (30:00)
6. You must keep [APP_NAME] open or timer pauses
7. Timer hits 0:00 → Apps unblock
8. Freedom!

**This is REAL enforcement.** Not honor system. Apps are literally blocked via iOS Screen Time API.

---

### **Which apps can be blocked?**

**You choose which apps to block.**

**Setup (one time):**
1. Settings → Blocked Apps
2. iOS Family Activity Picker appears
3. Select apps (Instagram, TikTok, Twitter, etc.)
4. Save selection

**These apps will be blocked when you're in jail.**

**Common choices:**
- Instagram (biggest time waster)
- TikTok (infinite scroll)
- Twitter (doomscrolling)
- Snapchat (constant checking)
- Facebook (old people jail)
- Games (Candy Crush, etc.)
- YouTube (can get lost for hours)

**You decide what distracts you most.**

---

### **Can I just delete [APP_NAME] to escape jail?**

**No.**

**iOS won't let you delete the app while Screen Time restrictions are active.**

**Try to delete:**
- iOS: "Cannot delete app while restrictions are active"
- Must complete jail time OR break jail (pay 2x fine)

**Also:**
- Apps remain blocked even if you restart phone
- Timer syncs to backend (can't cheat by resetting)

**You're locked in. Literally.**

---

### **What if I need to use a blocked app urgently?**

**"Break Jail" option.**

**How to break jail:**
1. In jail screen, tap "Break Jail"
2. Confirms: "Pay $10 to escape early?" (2x original fine)
3. Tap "Pay & Escape"
4. Payment processes
5. Apps unblock immediately

**When to use:**
- Emergency (need to call Uber)
- Work situation (boss DMs you)
- Genuine urgency

**Don't abuse:** Your pack sees you broke jail. Social pressure = don't do it often.

---

### **Does jail drain my battery?**

**Minimal battery usage.**

**What uses battery:**
- Screen on (showing timer)
- Background sync (every 10 seconds)
- Screen Time API (always-on)

**To minimize battery:**
- Dim screen
- Keep phone plugged in
- Don't let it run in background (timer pauses anyway)

**Typical drain:** 5-10% for 30-minute jail session. Not significant.

---

### **What if I put my phone down during jail?**

**Timer PAUSES.**

**How it works:**
1. Jail is running (timer at 28:45)
2. You lock phone or switch to another app
3. Jail detects you left
4. Timer pauses immediately
5. Notification: "⏸️ TIMER PAUSED - Get back to jail!"
6. You open [APP_NAME] again
7. Timer resumes from 28:45

**You can't game the system** by backgrounding the app and doing other things.

**Must actively serve your time.**

---

### **Can my friends see me in jail?**

**Yes. It's public in your pack feed.**

**What they see:**
- "[You] is in jail" (post)
- Timer countdown (live)
- Blocked apps list
- Why you're there (missed gym)

**They can:**
- React (💀🔥😂 emojis)
- Comment ("Serves you right lol")
- Watch the timer tick down

**Social pressure is the point.** Everyone knows you're serving time.

---

### **How do I turn off phone jail permissions?**

**iOS Settings:**
1. Settings → Screen Time
2. [APP_NAME]
3. "Remove Access"
4. Confirm

**This removes our ability to block apps.**

**What happens:**
- Currently serving jail? Apps unblock immediately
- Future jails? Can't activate (feature disabled)
- We'll prompt you to re-enable if you want phone jail

**You control your device.** We can't force Screen Time access. But without it, jail feature won't work.
